How these numbers are made

  • Every strategy runs on all stored NIFTY data. Option entries and exits use real 1-minute option candles from Groww, filled at the open of the minute after the signal bar closes.
  • Costs: slippage of 0.5% + 0.5 points per fill, brokerage, STT, exchange, SEBI, stamp duty and GST, as set in Backtest settings. One lot of the configured lot size, for every year.
  • If an option has no candle within 3 minutes of an entry or exit, the trade is skipped and counted. No price is estimated.
  • VWAP uses near-month NIFTY futures (the index has no volume). Confirmation strategies only enter when the indicator agrees; any opposite cross still exits.
  • Past results don't predict future results. Picking the best of 28 configurations after the fact overstates what you'd have earned; the walk-forward table shows the honest version.
