How the score works

  • Outcome: a signal is a hit if NIFTY reaches the target (2 × ATR) before the stop (1 × ATR), between the signal bar's close and 15:00. A minute touching both counts as a stop.
  • Score: a logistic model of the indicators. Every year is scored by a model trained only on earlier years, so the hit rates shown are out-of-sample.
  • The score is not a guaranteed probability. Compare it with the hit rate actually achieved in its band. Bands with few signals are unreliable.
  • Strong alert: score of at least 70, at least 3 supporting indicators, and at least 30 past signals in that band. Each signal appears once, so it can't repeat.
  • Not used: option-chain OI, IV and Greeks. Groww has no historical option-chain snapshots, so they can't be tested against past outcomes.
