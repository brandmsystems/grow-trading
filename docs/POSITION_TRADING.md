# Position Trading (multi-day NIFTY option ideas), 26 Nov 2026

*Built at the user's request. **Paper only**: nothing in `services/position/` or `api/position.py` imports `LiveBroker`, the live engine or any order function (a test checks the code, not the docs). It has its own virtual account and tables. The intraday, scalping, replay, live and automated-paper engines are untouched.*

Page: **Position Trading** (`/app/position`). Code: `services/position/` (`bars.py`, `studies.py`, `strategies.py`, `score.py`, `contracts.py`, `bsm.py`, `plan.py`, `analysis.py`, `backtest.py`, `account.py`, `settings.py`, `scheduler.py`), `api/position.py`, migration `0022_position_trading.sql` (four new tables, nothing existing touched; a verified `pg_dump` was taken first: `data/backups/pre_position_trading_2026-11-26.dump`, all tables, `candles_1m` rows excluded for size because the migration cannot touch them), research script `scripts/research_position.py`, tests `test_position_analysis.py`, `test_position_core.py`, `test_position_account.py`, `position-trading.test.tsx`.

> **Read this first.** The backtest on five years of stored data found **no strategy that is net positive after costs in training, validation and out-of-sample** (0 robust of 36 strategy x hold cells), and the pooled result is negative for every holding period. The page says so next to every idea. The 0-100 score counts sound conditions met; it is **not a probability of winning**, and it was not calibrated against results because no result supports one.

## What it does
1. **Multi-timeframe analysis on completed candles only.** Weekly, daily, 4-hour and 1-hour bars are built from the stored 1-minute NIFTY candles (`bars.py`). NSE session 09:15-15:30; the 4-hour bars are 09:15-13:15 and the 2h15 remainder 13:15-15:30. A daily bar needs all 375 minutes, a weekly bar needs its week to be over, and `completed(bars, asof)` drops anything still forming, so no value ever depends on a later candle (causality and repainting tests in `test_position_analysis.py`).
2. **Indicators** (`studies.py`, built on `strategy/indicators.py`, no duplicate calculations; ADX was added there): EMA 20/50/100/200, RSI, MACD, ADX with +DI/-DI, ATR, volume against the previous 20 bars (futures volume: the index has none), swing pivots (known only 3 bars after they form), prior 20-bar high/low, and a trend state. The **weekly trend uses EMA 10/20/50**, because EMA 100/200 on weekly bars need 2-4 years of weekly history and only five years are stored.
3. **Nine strategies** (`strategies.py`), each switched on/off and configured on its own (Settings card or `PUT /api/position/settings`): daily EMA trend-following, weekly trend + daily pullback, breakout and retest, support/resistance reversal, RSI divergence with trend confirmation, MACD momentum, ADX trend strength, volume-confirmed breakout, multi-timeframe confluence. Each decides on a completed daily bar, fires on the bar a condition *starts*, and is held to the weekly trend not being against it. Defaults are fixed starting values, never tuned.
4. **Contract analysis** (`contracts.py`, `bsm.py`): BUY CE and BUY PE are compared for the two nearest expiries a hold fits, five strikes (ITM 100 and 50, ATM, OTM 50 and 100). Quality 0-10: liquidity (OI and day volume from the decision day's stored candles), estimated theta, expiry fit and overnight gap risk. **Spread is not stored historically and is never invented** (it is checked live when Groww is connected). **IV, delta and theta are Black-Scholes *estimates* solved from the real premium** (assumed rate 6.5%, no dividends) and labelled "model estimate"; they are used for explanations and warnings only, never as a backtest input. The page explains why the chosen contract beats the alternatives.
5. **Score and decision** (`score.py`, 0-100): weekly trend 15, daily trend 15, 4-hour confirmation 10, momentum 15 (RSI 5, MACD 5, ADX 5), volume 10, support/resistance 10, volatility 10 (daily ATR % of price; India VIX is live context only, no history is stored), option contract quality 10, risk/reward 5. A missing value earns 0 points and is flagged. STRONG BUY CE/PE from 75, BUY from the configurable minimum (60), otherwise HOLD; **NO TRADE** when a gate fails (not enough daily bars, weekly trend not yet available or strongly against, no usable contract, a target that does not cover costs, sizing refuses). Strategies that disagree on a day give HOLD. The reasons and every component are shown.
6. **Plan** (`plan.py`): entry range, premium stop (-35%), Target 1 (+70%, 2R), Target 2 (+140%), trailing stop (after Target 1 the stop moves to the entry, then trails 25% below the best premium), NIFTY invalidation level, planned and forced exit dates, risk/reward after costs, maximum loss, capital required, lots and quantity from the existing `options.sizing.risk_size`, and warnings (theta per day and over a weekend, overnight gap risk, expiry, "events are not in the score"). The stop and Target 1 are the backtested rules; Target 2, the trail and the invalidation level are **management aids that were not backtested**, and the page says so.
7. **Context** (VIX, global markets, crude, FII/DII): read from the stored pre-market report of the day when there is one, shown as context, **never part of the score** (the pre-market validation found no open-to-close relation); otherwise "not available" with the reason.

## Historical backtest (`scripts/research_position.py run`, report in `data/exports/position_research.json`)
Rules, fixed before any result was looked at: decide on a completed daily bar; fill on the **next trading day at 09:20** at the stored 1-minute option candle's open; ATM strike of the decision close; the nearest expiry that leaves `ceil(1.5 x hold) + 2` calendar days; premium stop -35% (a gap through it fills at the open), target +70%, time exit at 15:15 on the hold's last trading day, forced exit at 15:15 the trading day before expiry, stop first when one candle touches both; 1 lot of the NSE lot size for that expiry; slippage and the full charge stack (`charges.round_trip`); one position per strategy and hold at a time. Holds: 3, 5, 10, 20 trading days. Splits: train 2022-23, validation 2024, out-of-sample 2025 onward; **no parameter is fitted, so these are hold-out periods, not re-fitted walk-forward results**. "Robust" = net positive in all three with at least 20 trades each.

**Result (stored data 11 Nov 2021 to 26 Nov 2026; 366 daily signals; 693 trades simulated, 740 not simulatable):**
| Hold | Trades | Net after all costs |
|---|---|---|
| 3 days | 347 | **-₹4,04,222** |
| 5 days | 247 | **-₹1,36,123** |
| 10 days | 87 | -₹16,075 |
| 20 days | 12 | -₹19,710 |

- **0 of 36 strategy x hold cells are robust.** Per strategy, every one is negative out of sample at the hold with the most trades (3 days): breakout and retest -₹80,944 over 25, mtf confluence -₹42,903 over 13, daily EMA trend -₹40,754 over 13, sr reversal -₹26,372 over 24.
- The few positive full-period cells are tiny and fail the period test: daily EMA trend 5 days +₹18,313 over 23 trades (out-of-sample -₹8,081 over 10), volume breakout 10 days +₹21,694 over 8, weekly pullback 5 days +₹13,295 over 12, weekly pullback 10 days +₹23,710 over 2. **Small samples; not evidence of an edge.**
- Win rates are mostly 24-43%; the average costs and slippage per trade are in the report (`costs`).

**Data limitations (the important part):**
- **740 trades could not be simulated and are counted, never filled with a made-up price.** The stored option candles cover only about 10 days (median; 90th percentile 20, maximum 26) before each expiry, so for 5-, 10- and 20-day holds most signals have no candle at entry (102, 245 and 278 trades) or no stored contract (2, 15, 67). **The 10- and 20-day results rest on the few cases that had data and are biased and unreliable**; the 3-day result is the only well-supported one.
- ATM +-10 strikes: a large move can leave the contract without candles.
- No historical IV, Greeks, bid-ask spread: not used and not invented. No history for VIX, global markets, crude or FII/DII, so the score cannot include them and the backtest does not.
- Weekly trend uses EMA 10/20/50 (see above); signals start about Nov 2022 (50 weekly bars), so the training period holds about 13 months.
- Exchange holidays are not in the data: planned exit dates are estimates; strikes are every 50 points.
- Not calibrated: with no positive out-of-sample result there is nothing to calibrate a probability from.

## Paper trading (`account.py`, tables `position_accounts`, `position_trades`)
A separate virtual account (default ₹5,00,000, resettable; earlier accounts and trades are kept). Multiple positions subject to: free capital, **maximum open positions** (3), **maximum portfolio risk** (sum of the open positions' maximum losses, 6% of capital), per-trade risk % (2%: one ATM lot with a 35% premium stop risks about ₹8,000, which 1% of ₹5,00,000 does not cover; `sizing.risk_size` with costs and slippage), maximum lots, and a unique signal key so **a signal is never entered twice**. Entries need a BUY / STRONG BUY idea; refusals give the exact reason; nothing is forced. Multi-day holding with **restart recovery**: everything is read back from the database. `manage` (every 5 minutes in market hours, once after 15:45, or "Check positions now") applies: premium stop-loss, Target 1 (stop to entry, then trailing), Target 2, time exit at 15:15, forced exit the trading day before expiry; a missing or stale (15 minutes) price changes nothing. P&L is net of charges and slippage; the account shows balance, equity, open P&L, exposure, open risk and drawdown. **Entry price:** a live Groww quote when reachable, otherwise the last traded premium of the decision day (marked on the position). Automatic entry (`auto_enter`, off) needs a validated-positive strategy, which none is, so it never fires today.
The daily decision (15:45) is stored once per bar in `position_decisions`, with reasons for every HOLD and NO TRADE, and never overwritten.

## API (`/api/position/...`)
`GET analysis` (60 s cache), `GET config`, `PUT settings` (validated as a whole), `GET account`, `POST account/reset`, `GET positions`, `POST open {direction}`, `POST manage`, `GET decisions`, `POST decide`, `GET backtest`, `GET chart?tf=D|W|4h|1h`. No route returns Groww account data (funds, margin, profile, positions, orders); the only Groww read is one quote of a contract's premium.

## Limits
Not verified in a live market session; the decision aid has not shown an edge; weekly EMA 100/200 are not used; the price in the paper entry may be the decision day's last premium when the market is closed; events (RBI, budget, results) are warned about, not scored; the 1-hour and 4-hour frames feed the trend labels and the 4-hour confirmation but are not backtested as entry timeframes.
